# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want participation in Not Codex to be useful, respectful, and safe. Contributors,
maintainers, and community members are expected to treat one another with patience and
professionalism regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, and project direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
personal attacks, doxxing, and knowingly publishing secrets or security details are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation,
or ban accounts when behavior is harmful to the project or its community. Enforcement
decisions may consider context, severity, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/MaTriXy). For security vulnerabilities, follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially representing
Not Codex elsewhere.
